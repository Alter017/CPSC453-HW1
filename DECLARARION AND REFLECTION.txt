c. CRITICAL EVALUATION:
How did you verify the accuracy of the outputs generated by the AI? 
Describe any instances where the tool gave incorrect, suboptimal, 
or misleading guidance, and how you corrected it.

For the most part, the questions I was asking to ChatGPT needed
solutions that either worked or not. Meaning that if ChatGPT was
right, I was able to prove it, and so I was when it was wrong.

For example, when fixing the error in this line:
"#include <SFML/Graphics.hpp>"; at first, ChatGPT told me to
follow these steps:

1. Press Ctrl+Shift+P.
2. Run CMake: Configure.
3. Choose the compiler/toolchain when prompted.
4. Wait for CMake configuration to finish.
5. Run CMake: Build.

And told me the error would be gone after completing them. However,
it did not work. So I could observe that the model GPT-5.6 Luna was
wrong. Nevertheless, I prompted this model with the output I
received after configuring and building the program and then it
realized that the problem was actually that my CMake version was
too old for the program. And after downloading and installing an
appropriate version, I finally confirmed that the model GPT-5.6
Luna was right.
